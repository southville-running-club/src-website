Direction A: Community and warmth

Mood: a Tuesday night on the harbourside. Friendly faces, the walk-to-run group setting off, drinks after. It leads with people and reassurance, which is what a nervous first-timer needs, and it's the direction most like the club's own voice.

Type: Bricolage Grotesque for headings (warm, slightly quirky, reads like a hand-painted club banner) with Nunito Sans for body text (open humanist letterforms, very legible at small sizes on a phone).

Restraint: one big photo in the hero, then flat sections with plenty of space. Cards only where something is clickable, one testimonial rather than three, and yellow kept for the pace highlight and the Nightingale Nightmare page.

Your first night at SRC

Everything you need to turn up on a Tuesday and enjoy it. No sign-up and no experience needed: just 50p and a pair of running shoes.

On the night

  1. 6.00pmMeet at the Southbank Club, Dean Lane, BS3 1DB.
  2. By 6.10pmBe there for the pre-run announcements.
  3. 6.10pmEach group leader (GL) says what their group is doing tonight.
  4. 6.15pmChoose a group by pace and off you go.
  5. About 7.15pmBack at base. Drinks optional, but popular.

About group leaders

GLs are experienced volunteers, and some are England Athletics qualified run leaders. Some run full training sessions, others act as guides. They do their best to get everyone back, especially new runners.

Because GLs are volunteers, the groups on offer vary a little from week to week.

Find my group

Groups are organised by pace, in minutes per mile. Enter a recent 5K time and we'll suggest where to start.

Never timed a 5K? Start with the social group or walk-to-run on a Tuesday.

Plus the walk-to-run group on Tuesdays, and the social group: about 4 miles round the harbourside, with rests.

What the sessions look like

Most groups run for about an hour. Tuesday sessions range from 4 to 11 miles and might be:

  • Hill repeats
  • Fartlek
  • Pyramids
  • Kenyan intervals
  • Stamina intervals
  • Exercises

Routes cover all of Bristol: mostly roads in winter, Ashton Court and Leigh Woods in summer and at weekends.

Running etiquette

  • Dress for the conditions. No ninja black in the dark.
  • Follow your group leader's instructions.
  • Run no more than two abreast.
  • Be courteous to everyone else on the pavement.
  • Keep left, overtake on the right.
  • Call out hazards: pedestrians, cyclists, dogs, potholes, cars.
  • Dropping out? Tell your GL, so nobody goes looking for you.
  • Enjoy it, and tell us how it went.

FAQs

I'm worried about being left behind

We never leave anyone behind. Group leaders wait, loop back and count everyone home. On Tuesdays there's a walk-to-run group for complete beginners.

How far do you run?

Most groups run for about an hour, covering 4 to 7 or 8 miles. The social group does about 4 miles round the harbourside, with rests.

Where do you run?

All over Bristol. In winter we mostly stay on roads. In summer and at weekends we head for Ashton Court and Leigh Woods.

Which night is best for beginners?

Tuesday. It's our busiest night, with the most groups and the walk-to-run group.

What should I wear?

Decent running shoes. Reflective or hi-viz kit is compulsory on dark nights, so no ninja black. We have a few spares to borrow.

Running terminology

Intervals

Repeated fast efforts with recovery in between, such as 6 × 800m with a 90-second jog.

Fartlek

Swedish for 'speed play': unstructured bursts of faster running mixed with easy running.

Hills

Run up a hill hard, jog or walk down to recover, repeat. Builds strength and form.

Pyramids

Efforts that build up and then come back down, such as 1, 2, 3, 2, 1 minutes.

Recovery runs

Easy, chatty runs that help your body absorb harder sessions.

Stamina

Longer, sustained efforts at a strong but controlled pace.

Tempo run

A 'comfortably hard' continuous run, near the pace you could hold for an hour.

Threshold run

Running just below the point where your legs start to fill with lactate faster than you clear it.

VO2 max

The most oxygen your body can use. Short, hard intervals help raise it.

Warm up

Ten to fifteen minutes of easy running and drills before anything fast.

    Questions people ask

    Who are we?

    A friendly club founded in 2007. Some of us run or walk 5km, some enjoy social runs round South Bristol, and some are chasing race times.

    What happens after the run?

    Drinks at the Southbank Club, plus regular socials. Keep an eye on Facebook.

    Do I need hi-viz?

    Yes, from autumn [September or October?] until the clocks go forward in spring. We have spares to borrow.

    Can I cycle there?

    Yes. Bikes can be locked in the garden behind the Southbank Club.

    Where do I leave my bag?

    There's bag storage from 6.00 to 6.15pm, but it isn't secure. The Southbank Club rents lockers for a £5 deposit.

    Do people race?

    Some run for fun and fitness. Many race on road and trail, in the UK and abroad.

    How do I keep up with club news?

    Join the monthly newsletter. Kit pre-orders open a few times a year.
