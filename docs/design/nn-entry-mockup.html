Race day

Race HQ: Ashton Park School, Blackmoors Lane, BS3 2JL — free on-site parking, toilets, shelter, bag drop and changing. Please lift-share; no on-street parking, respect the neighbours.

  • 09:15Registration opens at HQ — collect your number.
  • 10:15Race briefing at HQ.
  • 10:30Walk to the start together — the start and finish are not at HQ. Bag drop at both.
  • 10:45Group warm-up at the start.
  • 11:00Go.
  • AfterFree hot drinks and cake back at HQ (charity tin out), prizegiving after the last finisher.

Bringing supporters?

Best spectating is at the start/finish and at the top of Nightingale Valley via the gate on North Road, BS8 3PL. Spectator parking on Brunel Lock Road, then walk over Ashton Avenue Bridge. Fancy dress applies to spectators too — no exemptions.
