The course

COURSE MAP

Towpath, up Nightingale Valley, and through Leigh Woods. A tough off-road run with plenty of elevation — uneven ground, rocks and roots, and slippery after rain.

Race information

Race morning

09:15Registration opens. Numbers are collected at HQ.
10:15Race briefing at HQ.
10:30We leave on foot and walk to the start together.
11:00Race starts.

Getting there and parking

Free parking on site, and toilets and shelter at HQ. The car park is large but not infinite — share lifts where you can. There is no on-street parking in the area, so please respect our neighbours.

[ COPY TO COME — KAYLEIGH ]

What to wear

Trail shoes are a good option if you have them. Road shoes are fine, just take extra care. Flip-flops would be disastrous. Changing areas and a bag drop are at HQ, and there is a second bag drop at the start.

No headphones of any type during the race.

On the day

There is a group warm-up at the start. One water station on the route and another at the finish, with marshals throughout. Pack something warm for after — it is worth staying to cheer the rest of the field in.

Prizes and fancy dress

First, second and third female and male. First Vet 40, Vet 50 and Vet 60, female and male. Three prizes for best fancy dress, and one for the best marshalling station as voted by runners. A medal for every finisher.

For the fancy dress competition, collect your number and then find the photographers upstairs at HQ. Prizegiving is at HQ once the last runner is in.

Cake

Free tea, coffee and hot chocolate back at HQ, and cake baked by club volunteers. There is a donation tin — past years have funded some very good local causes.

Spooktators

Friends and family are very welcome. The start and finish area is the best spot, and the top of Nightingale Valley is easy to reach through the gate on North Road, BS8 3PL. Park on Brunel Lock Road and walk over Ashton Avenue Bridge.
